Charts from your inventory history

Each saved quantity is stored with its row, column, initials, and date. That history can support charts and trends when you decide to add them.

Supported by existing database

feature

On-par levels and alerts

Set an on-par range for each item: the quantity you want on hand and how many days can pass between counts. Cells that fall below par or go uncounted too long are highlighted in the grid, and an alert can notify the people you choose.

Builds on the saved history

feature

Weekly digest and alert emails

A scheduled job could send a weekly summary of inventory changes to your team, and the on-par alerts described under Analytics could email the people you choose.
